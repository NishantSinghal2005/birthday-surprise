I'm genuinely sorry, Khushi Ji. 🤍

I know ki meri kuch baaton, mere actions aur meri mistakes ki wajah se aapko bura laga hoga. Kabhi meri wajah se aap hurt hui hongi, kabhi maine unnecessary problems create ki hongi, aur kabhi meri kisi baat ne aapka mood ya peace kharab kiya hoga. Un sab cheezon ke liye, I'm genuinely sorry.

Mujhe afsos hai un moments ka jab mujhe aapki baat samajhni chahiye thi, lekin main nahi samajh paaya. Un moments ka bhi jab mujhe thoda calmly sochna chahiye tha, lekin maine aisa nahi kiya. Aur agar kabhi meri wajah se aapko laga ki aapki feelings ko samjha nahi ja raha, toh I'm really sorry for that too.

Main ye nahi keh raha ki meri har galti ka koi reason tha, ya jo kuch hua usse bas ek sorry bolkar theek kiya ja sakta hai. Galti meri thi toh uski responsibility lena bhi meri hi responsibility hai. Main past ko change nahi kar sakta, lekin apne behaviour se seekh zaroor sakta hoon.

Kabhi-kabhi insaan bina chahe bhi kisi ko hurt kar deta hai, but iska matlab ye nahi ki hurt hua hi nahi. Intentions chahe jo bhi rahe hon, agar meri kisi baat ya action se aapko takleef hui, toh mujhe us baat ko seriously lena chahiye tha. I'm sorry, Khushi Ji.

Aap meri friend ho, aur main genuinely value karta hoon hamari friendship ko. Isliye mujhe bura lagta hai ki kabhi meri hi wajah se cheezein difficult hui hongi. Dosti mein comfort, respect aur understanding honi chahiye — unnecessary stress nahi. I know ki mujhe kuch situations mein better handle karna chahiye tha.

Main ye apology isliye nahi kar raha ki aap mujhe turant maaf kar do, ya sab kuch bhool jao. Aapko jo feel hua, woh aapki feelings hain, aur unki apni importance hai. Aap par koi pressure nahi hai ki aap is message ka reply do, mujhe reassure karo, ya abhi sab kuch normal kar do.

Bas main chahta hoon ki aapko pata ho ki mujhe apni galtiyon ka ehsaas hai, aur main unhe repeat na karne ki honestly koshish karna chahta hoon. Sirf words se nahi, balki apne actions se bhi better behave karna chahta hoon. I know ki trust aur comfort words se nahi, time aur behaviour se build hote hain.

Khushi Ji, agar kabhi meri wajah se aapko disappointment hui, stress mila, ya aapko woh feel karna pada jo aap deserve nahi karti thi, toh dil se maafi chahta hoon. Kaash main un moments ko better handle kar paaya hota. Main past ko undo nahi kar sakta, but I can learn from it and do better going forward.

Aur ek baat, main nahi chahta ki meri apology ki wajah se aapke birthday ki happiness kam ho. Aaj ka din aapka hai, aur aap deserve karti ho ki aap khush raho, smile karo, apne favourite moments enjoy karo, aur genuinely special feel karo. Ye page bas ek baat honestly accept karne ke liye hai jo mujhe aapse kehni thi.

I'm sorry for the times I hurt you, for the things I could have handled better, and for the problems I created. Main perfect nahi hoon, but mujhe apni mistakes se seekhna chahiye — aur main wahi karna chahta hoon.

I genuinely wish ki aapki life mein bahut saari happiness aaye, aapko peace mile, aap apne aap par proud feel karo, aur aapke aas-paas aise log hon jo aapko samjhein aur respect karein. Aapke liye dil se achha hi chahta hoon, Khushi Ji. 🤍

Once again, I'm truly sorry. No excuses, no expectations — bas ek sincere apology aur ye hope ki main apni mistakes se kuch achha seekh paun. Thank you for reading this, Khushi Ji.

Dil se maafi chahta hoon. 🤍
